    Daily snapshot

    Sources. Home fixtures for the 494 clubs in the register, read from ESPN's public feed and rebuilt each morning; each result carries the time its fixture was last read. Kickoff is shown in the ground's own time zone, with your time beside it when they differ. Distances are straight-line from the place you chose to the ground, not travelling distance. Your location, if you use it, stays in the browser — it is never sent anywhere or written into the address bar. The League of Ireland is not carried by that feed and comes from a hand-kept file, so it updates less often. Tickets links go to the club’s own ticket page, which we hold per club rather than per match, so it cannot tell you whether a particular game has sold out. Where we have no official page, ESPN sometimes carries a listing on a third-party resale marketplace: those are marked Resale, are priced in US dollars, and are not the club’s box office. We strip the tracking parameters from them and earn nothing from either link.
